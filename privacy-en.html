Privacy Policy

DYP: drag your plan and actualize it (hereinafter “DYP”) · Effective date 2026-11-04

In short: DYP itself collects no personal data. App records and calendar integration are handled on your device and are never sent to DYP servers or analytics tools. Only if you turn on iCloud sync are your records stored in your own iCloud account, which the developer cannot access.

1Data We Collect and Why

None. DYP has no servers, accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs. The app’s only network activity is the optional iCloud sync you turn on yourself (Section 5), which stores data solely in your own iCloud account provided by Apple. There is neither a purpose nor a means for DYP to collect, use, or share personal data.

2Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, these records except voice recordings are also stored in your iCloud account (Section 5). The developer has no access to either:

  • Time blocks (planned and actual), goals, and reflection notes
  • One-line notes and questions you leave for yourself
  • Voice recordings (m4a files) and the names you give them
  • Event titles and times read on-device from the calendar you select, plus DYP plans written to that calendar
  • App preferences (such as highlighter color)

If you add a Home Screen widget, the app stores today's plans (times and titles) and Top 3 goals (titles and completion) in an app-only shared container (App Group) so the widget on the same device can display them. This information never leaves your device. Widget content may be visible to others wherever you place the widget.

3Microphone

The microphone is used solely to record your voice notes. Recordings are saved on your device and never leave it. Every text feature works without microphone permission.

4Calendar Access

When you use calendar integration, DYP reads event titles and times from the calendar you select on your device only, so it can show your schedule, and writes your DYP plans to the calendar you select. This information is never sent to DYP servers, analytics tools, or over the network.

Full calendar access is needed so DYP can read events from your selected calendar and write your plans there. You can revoke access at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars. Any syncing performed by the calendar account itself follows your settings and the calendar provider’s privacy policy.

5iCloud Sync and Device Backup

iCloud sync (optional, off by default). If you turn on iCloud sync in the app's settings, your time blocks, goals and review text, notes and questions are stored in your own private database in Apple CloudKit so that your iPhone and Mac (DYP Companion) signed in to the same Apple Account stay in sync. Record contents are kept in CloudKit encrypted fields. Voice recordings and calendar link data are not uploaded. This data uses your iCloud storage and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer cannot access it. You can turn sync off in the app's settings at any time.

iCloud device backup. If you use iOS device backup (iCloud Backup), app data may be included in your own backup. This is standard Apple functionality; the developer cannot access your backups.

6In-App Purchases

Payments for paid features are processed by Apple’s App Store. The developer never sees your payment details; purchase verification happens on-device. See App Store & Privacy for how Apple handles payment data.

7Sharing with Third Parties

None. No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. When iCloud sync is on, storage happens under your own iCloud relationship with Apple; the developer does not engage Apple to process your data.

8Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app, and deleting the app deletes its local data. DYP plan events already written to your calendar account are not automatically deleted when the app is removed; delete those from the Calendar app if needed. If you used iCloud sync, DYP data in iCloud remains after deleting the app; you can delete it in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage (or the same menu in System Settings on Mac) by choosing DYP. The developer holds no copy anywhere.

9Your Rights

Because all data lives on your device, you can view, edit, and erase it at any time directly in the app. There is no developer-held data against which to exercise access, correction, deletion, or restriction requests. Questions about this policy are welcome at the contact below and will be answered in good faith.

10Security

Data is stored inside the iOS app sandbox, inaccessible to other apps, and protected by iOS device encryption when your device is locked.

11Children’s Privacy

DYP collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
